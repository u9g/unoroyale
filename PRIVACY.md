# Uno Royale Privacy Policy

Uno Royale does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely offline on your device.

The only things the app sends anywhere: an anonymous counter when a game starts or finishes (players, winner, duration), and a periodic check for updated game files. Both carry a random ID created on first launch — it identifies your install, not you.

If you send in-app feedback, we receive your message and the same random ID, plus your email only if you enter one.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.

For questions, contact [uno@u9g.dev](mailto:uno@u9g.dev).
