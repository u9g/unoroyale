# Uno Royale Privacy Policy

Uno Royale does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game runs entirely offline on your device.

The only thing the app sends anywhere is an anonymous counter when a game starts or finishes: the number of players at the table, whether you or a computer player won, and how long the game took. No identifiers of any kind (no device ID, account, name, or IP-based tracking) are attached, so these counts cannot be linked to you.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included.

For questions, contact [uno@u9g.dev](mailto:uno@u9g.dev).
